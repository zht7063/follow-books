An index is not a timeline

The old seed describes a very large posting history and substantial gaps. This project does not attempt to complete it. X references use URLs only in this reader and search, are labelled unread, and were not re-fetched. Derived metadata retains seed titles, tags and historical verification notes; these do not establish a new reading. We exclude their old summaries from this reader and its search text.

A date beside a reference reports what the seed recorded. For the account entry it is an account-creation date, not an essay publication date. We preserve that explanation so the presence of a date does not create a false chronology of ideas.
